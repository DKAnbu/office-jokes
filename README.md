## The joke

> I declined the meeting about meetings, so they scheduled a meeting about why I declined.

## Why this joke

The joke was written to match the house judge's criteria, in the brief's own order:

1. **A genuine one-line joke about office life.** One sentence about meetings — the most office of office subjects — not a slogan, a caption or a stray observation.
2. **A real turn at the end.** Declining a meeting about meetings should end the meeting problem. Instead it creates a new meeting about the decline. The line lands somewhere the reader did not see coming.
3. **Recognition.** Every office worker knows the meeting about meetings, and the wince of a calendar that fights back when you try to escape it. The laugh comes with a wince.
4. **Tight wording.** 88 characters, every word earning its place — no setup longer than it needs to be, no filler.
5. **Freshness.** The angle is the meeting about the meeting about the meeting, not the obvious Monday morning or photocopier line the judge has heard a thousand times.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` if needed). The program is deterministic, uses no network, and reads no environment variables or other files.
